# Dispatcher

Work one station's platforms: send Train Ready To Start, close the doors and give each train the Right Away when its signal is clear.

## Ranks

| Rank | Short | Job XP needed |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee Dispatcher | TD | 0 |
| Qualified Dispatcher | QD | 300 |
| Senior Dispatcher | SD | 1500 |
| Dispatch Instructor | DI | Awarded by staff |
| Station Supervisor | STS | Awarded by staff |
