# Train Operator

Drive passenger trains: take a service, prepare the train, stop at every station and keep to time.

## Ranks

| Rank | Short | Job XP needed |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee Operator | TO | 0 |
| Qualified Operator | QO | 300 |
| Senior Operator | SO | 1500 |
| Operations Instructor | OI | Awarded by staff |
| Operations Supervisor | OS | Awarded by staff |
