# Maintenance

Take maintenance trains out to faulty signals, track and platforms, protect the work area and make repairs.

## Ranks

| Rank | Short | Job XP needed |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee Maintenance Worker | TMW | 0 |
| Qualified Maintenance Worker | QMW | 300 |
| Senior Maintenance Worker | SMW | 1500 |
| Maintenance Instructor | MI | Awarded by staff |
| Maintenance Supervisor | MS | Awarded by staff |
