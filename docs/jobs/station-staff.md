# Station Staff

Keep the stations clean: pick up litter, mop spills, empty the bins and take the trash out, then ride a train to the next station that needs you.

## Ranks

| Rank | Short | Job XP needed |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee Station Assistant | TSA | 0 |
| Station Assistant | SA | 300 |
| Senior Station Assistant | SSA | 1500 |
| Station Training Officer | STO | Awarded by staff |
| Station Manager | SM | Awarded by staff |
