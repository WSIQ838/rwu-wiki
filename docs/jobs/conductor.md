# Conductor

Ride a passenger train: open the doors on the platform side, manage boarding and tell the operator when it is ready to depart.

## Ranks

| Rank | Short | Job XP needed |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee Guard | TG | 0 |
| Qualified Guard | QG | 300 |
| Senior Guard | SG | 1500 |
| Guard Instructor | GI | Awarded by staff |
| Passenger Operations Supervisor | POS | Awarded by staff |
