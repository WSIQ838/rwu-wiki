# Jobs

There are 6 jobs. Each has its own XP and five ranks; the top two ranks are awarded by staff.

| Job | What you do |
|---|---|
| [Train Operator](train-operator.md) | Drive passenger trains: take a service, prepare the train, stop at every station and keep to time. |
| [Conductor](conductor.md) | Ride a passenger train: open the doors on the platform side, manage boarding and tell the operator when it is ready to depart. |
| [Signaler](signaler.md) | Run a signal box: set routes, move points and keep trains safely apart on the live track diagram. |
| [Dispatcher](dispatcher.md) | Work one station's platforms: send Train Ready To Start, close the doors and give each train the Right Away when its signal is clear. |
| [Station Staff](station-staff.md) | Keep the stations clean: pick up litter, mop spills, empty the bins and take the trash out, then ride a train to the next station that needs you. |
| [Maintenance](maintenance.md) | Take maintenance trains out to faulty signals, track and platforms, protect the work area and make repairs. |
