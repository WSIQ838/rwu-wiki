# Class 458

The **Class 458** is a electric train with a top speed of 100 mph. It runs for Coastline, Express, JetRail and MetroLink.

## Sizes

| Formation | Cars | Length | Seats | Weight | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 cars | 4 | 301 studs (84 m) | 280 | 164 t | 1080 kW |
| 8 cars (2 units) | 8 | 602 studs (169 m) | 560 | 328 t | 2160 kW |

## Who can drive it

Any Train Operator can drive it.

A train that is longer than a platform stops with its front at the far end of the platform and opens only the doors of the cars that are alongside.
