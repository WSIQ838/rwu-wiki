# HST

The **HST** is a diesel train with a top speed of 125 mph. It runs for Coastline, Express, JetRail and MetroLink.

## Who can drive it

It needs the Qualified Operator rank (300 Operator XP).

A train that is longer than a platform stops with its front at the far end of the platform and opens only the doors of the cars that are alongside.
