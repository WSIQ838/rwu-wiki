# Class 994

The **Class 994** is a electric train with a top speed of 100 mph. It runs for Coastline, Express, JetRail and MetroLink.

## Sizes

| Formation | Cars | Length | Seats | Weight | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 cars | 2 | 199 studs (56 m) | 150 | 90 t | 840 kW |
| 4 cars (2 units) | 4 | 398 studs (111 m) | 300 | 180 t | 1680 kW |
| 6 cars (3 units) | 6 | 598 studs (167 m) | 450 | 270 t | 2520 kW |

## Who can drive it

Any Train Operator can drive it.

A train that is longer than a platform stops with its front at the far end of the platform and opens only the doors of the cars that are alongside.
