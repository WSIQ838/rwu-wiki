# Class 180

The **Class 180** is a diesel train with a top speed of 125 mph. It runs for Coastline, Express, JetRail and MetroLink.

## Sizes

| Formation | Cars | Length | Seats | Weight | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 cars | 5 | 422 studs (118 m) | 300 | 250 t | 2800 kW |
| 10 cars (2 units) | 10 | 844 studs (236 m) | 600 | 500 t | 5600 kW |

| Company | Liveries |

## Who can drive it

Any Train Operator can drive it.

A train that is longer than a platform stops with its front at the far end of the platform and opens only the doors of the cars that are alongside.
