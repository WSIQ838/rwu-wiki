# Class 156

The **Class 156** is a diesel train with a top speed of 75 mph. It runs for Coastline, Express, JetRail and MetroLink.

## Sizes

| Formation | Cars | Length | Seats | Weight | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 cars | 2 | 165 studs (46 m) | 148 | 76 t | 426 kW |
| 4 cars (2 units) | 4 | 331 studs (93 m) | 296 | 152 t | 852 kW |
| 6 cars (3 units) | 6 | 496 studs (139 m) | 444 | 228 t | 1278 kW |

## Who can drive it

Any Train Operator can drive it.

A train that is longer than a platform stops with its front at the far end of the platform and opens only the doors of the cars that are alongside.
