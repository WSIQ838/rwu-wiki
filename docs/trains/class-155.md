# Class 155

The **Class 155** is a diesel train with a top speed of 75 mph. It runs for Coastline, Express, JetRail and MetroLink.

## Sizes

| Formation | Cars | Length | Seats | Weight | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 cars | 2 | 166 studs (46 m) | 152 | 77 t | 426 kW |
| 4 cars (2 units) | 4 | 333 studs (93 m) | 304 | 154 t | 852 kW |
| 6 cars (3 units) | 6 | 500 studs (140 m) | 456 | 231 t | 1278 kW |

## Who can drive it

Any Train Operator can drive it.

A train that is longer than a platform stops with its front at the far end of the platform and opens only the doors of the cars that are alongside.
