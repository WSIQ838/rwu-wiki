# Driving a route

This is how the Train Operator's Drive menu works.

## Picking a train and a route

1. Open the Drive menu and pick a **company**: MetroLink, Express, JetRail or Coastline.
2. Pick a **train**: first the class, then the livery, then how many cars.
3. Pick a **route**, then the **station** to start from.
4. Pick the **yard** the train comes out of. The nearest yard to your station is listed first.
5. Pick how the route is run: **Regular** (all stops), **Rapid** (every other stop) or **Express** (major stations only).

All trains and all routes are open to every player. The HST also needs the Qualified Operator rank (300 Operator XP).

## Out on the line

1. The train comes out of the yard with you in the cab, not in service, and you drive it empty to your starting station.
2. Stopping there earns XP and you are asked whether to start the route.
3. The train then works the route towards the far end, stopping at the stations for the type you picked. The doors open and close by themselves and the starting signal clears by itself.
4. At the last station you get the XP for the service and are asked whether to continue back the other way. At a terminus you change ends once the doors are closed.
5. If you leave the cab while out on the line, or end your duty, the train is removed.

## Long trains at short platforms

A train that is longer than a platform stops with its front at the far end of the platform. Only the doors of the cars that are alongside the platform open. See the [train pages](../trains/index.md) for the length of each formation and the [station pages](../stations/index.md) for platform lengths.

## Menu keys

| Key | What it opens |
|---|---|
| J | Jobs |
| F1 | Keys |

While you are in a job, menu screens have a **Close** button that closes the screen and keeps your job. The Menu button takes you back to the main menu and ends your job.
